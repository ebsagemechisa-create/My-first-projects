How to make Coffe

Needed Ingredients

Ethiopian-Style Coffe

  • Green coffee beans
  • Water
  • Sugar, optional
  • Some times rue, or other spices /aromatics , depending on household

Simple Coffee

  • Coffee 1-2 teaspoons(ground coffee or instant)
  • Hot Water about 200 ml
  • Sugar 1-2 teaspoons, optional
  • Milk 50 - 100 ml, optional

Instructions Section

  1. 🔥Boil The Water heat about 200ml until hot/building
  2. ☕Add Coffe put 1-2 tea spoons of coffee into your cup
  3. ♨Add Hot Waterpour the hot water into the cup
  4. 🥄Stirr Well until the coffe dissolves
  5. 🥛Add Sugar According to your taste
  6. 🧊Add milk if you want a smoother , cream coffee
